# LaunchStack CLI governance

LaunchStack CLI is currently a Zivora Labs-maintained project. The known lead is Williams Ashibuogwu, whose programme responsibilities are recorded in [OpenChain records](docs/openchain/PROGRAM-RECORD-2026-11-18.md).

Issues and pull requests are the default venue for public technical decisions. Maintainers evaluate correctness, secure development, licensing, backward compatibility, tests, documentation and long-term support costs. Vulnerabilities are handled privately.

The [default-branch ruleset](https://github.com/wbizmo/launchstack-cli/rules/21824160) mandates two approvals and code-owner review, but explicitly permits repository-role bypass. Actual, independent reviews must be verified separately before satisfying OpenSSF Gold.

**Continuity limitation:** The project has not established a verified second independent significant contributor or a demonstrated bus factor of two. Recruit qualified maintainers, establish documented access recovery and verify continuity before claiming those Gold criteria. No future maintainer or legal reviewer may be invented in the badge application.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for submission and review requirements.
